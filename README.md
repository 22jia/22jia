## 🧭 How I approach product work

- **Start with the task.** Define who needs help, what they are trying to finish, and where the workflow breaks.
- **Build the smallest useful loop.** Make the core journey tangible before expanding the feature list.
- **Make AI inspectable.** Show evidence, surface missing context, and design where a person takes over.
- **Separate demos from impact.** Working prototypes are evidence of implementation; adoption and business outcomes need their own validation.

These repositories include prototypes and product case studies at different stages. Each project explains what is implemented, what is illustrative, and what remains to be validated.
